Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026

ToneGen does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

What the app does not do

  • It has no accounts and asks for no personal information.
  • It makes no network connections. Nothing leaves your device.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking code, and no third-party SDKs.
  • It does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts, or photos. It only produces audio output.

Data stored on your device

None. ToneGen does not save settings or files.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If a future version changes any of the above, this page will be updated and the effective date revised before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised by opening an issue on GitHub.
