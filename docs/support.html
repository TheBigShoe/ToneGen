Support

For help, bug reports, or feature requests, open an issue on GitHub. A free GitHub account is required. Please include your device model, iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem occurred.

Common questions

I pressed play and hear nothing

Check the device volume and that neither channel is muted. If the footer shows “AUDIO OUTPUT UNAVAILABLE”, another app or a call is holding the audio output; end it and press play again.

The tone stopped when I unplugged my headphones

This is deliberate. ToneGen stops rather than send a test tone to the built-in speaker unexpectedly.

How loud is it?

Levels are shown in dBFS relative to digital full scale. Actual loudness depends on your device volume and whatever is connected. Start with the volume low: sustained tones at high level can damage hearing and loudspeakers.

Does it work with a USB or Bluetooth audio interface?

Yes. ToneGen plays through whichever output iOS has selected.
