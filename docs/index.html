ToneGen

Stereo test tone generator for iPhone and iPad

ToneGen is a precision signal generator for audio engineers. Set waveform, frequency, level and phase independently for the left and right channels, or link them and drive both together.

  • Sine, square, triangle and sawtooth waves, plus white and pink noise
  • 20 Hz to 20 kHz on a logarithmic scale, with note name and cents readout
  • Levels in dBFS, with per-channel mute and a master gain
  • Adjustable phase offset between channels
  • Keeps generating with the screen locked

ToneGen is free and open source under the MIT licence.
